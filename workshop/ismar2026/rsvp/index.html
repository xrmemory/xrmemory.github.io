Discussion themes

01

Defining XRMemory and Its Scope

What gives XRMemory a distinct identity, and whose experience does it represent?

Explore discussion prompts
  • What gives XRMemory a distinct identity?
  • How is XRMemory different from experience recording, digital twins, simulations, and immersive re-experience?
  • Whose perspective determines whether a reconstructed memory is authentic?
02

Capturing and Representing XRMemory

What information should be captured, and what context should accompany it?

Explore discussion prompts
  • Which data are essential, optional, or inappropriate to capture?
  • What contextual metadata and provenance should accompany a memory?
  • What counts as sufficient capture for later use?
03

Reconstructing and Experiencing XRMemory

How can reconstructed memories adapt while making their limits clear?

Explore discussion prompts
  • What should be adapted, inferred, or generated in a reconstructed memory?
  • How should a system communicate uncertainty and AI-generated content?
  • How do physical fidelity and experiential authenticity differ?
04

Preserving and Governing XRMemory

How should XRMemory be controlled, cared for, and preserved over time?

Explore discussion prompts
  • Who should own XRMemory, and who can access, modify, or share it?
  • How should consent, privacy, and stewardship be managed, including deletion and the right to forget?
  • What infrastructure and standards are needed for long-term preservation?
