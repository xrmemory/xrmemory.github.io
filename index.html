The Future of Spatial Memory

XRMemory investigates how memories can move beyond passive photos and videos toward immersive, interactive, and reconfigurable records of lived spatial experience.

The series brings together researchers across XR, AI, graphics, computer vision, spatial computing, large language models, and HCI to study the technological and human foundations of memory capture and adaptive replay.

Research Themes

Capture and Reconstruction

Methods for recording, segmenting, reconstructing, and structuring personal experiences in immersive spaces.

Adaptive Playback

Interactive replay systems that adapt to users, contexts, viewpoints, virtual agents, and changing memory needs.

Human Factors

Perceptual, cognitive, emotional, ethical, and trust-related questions in remembered and replayed XR experiences.
